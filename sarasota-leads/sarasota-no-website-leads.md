# Sarasota, FL — Businesses With No (or a Weak) Website

Lead list for web-design outreach. **45 verified leads** compiled 2026-07-25.

Every business below was individually checked: searched `"<name> Sarasota website"` and inspected results. A business only made the list if it has **no owned domain**, is **social/booking-app only**, or its **domain is dead/ancient**. Dead domains were confirmed by direct fetch (DNS failure). Roughly 60+ additional candidates were checked and *excluded* because they already have working websites.

## How to read "Web Presence"

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **DEAD DOMAIN** | Had a website; the domain no longer resolves but is still linked from Google/Yelp/BBB. Hottest leads — they already valued a website once. |
| **NO WEBSITE** | Nothing but Yelp/Google/directory listings. |
| **SOCIAL ONLY** | Facebook and/or Instagram is their entire owned presence. |
| **BOOKING-APP ONLY** | Lives on Booksy/Fresha/Vagaro/Thumbtack — pays a platform instead of owning a site. |
| **FREE-BUILDER PAGE** | Free square.site / godaddysites.com subdomain — no real domain. |

## Methodology & honest limitations

- **This is not literally "every" business in Sarasota without a good website** — no such registry exists, and Sarasota County has tens of thousands of businesses. This is a verified sample across the categories where no-website businesses concentrate.
- Sources swept: Yelp, Google, Facebook, Instagram, Booksy, Fresha, Vagaro, Thumbtack, Nextdoor, Roaming Hunger, sarasotalocal.com, sarasotaflbest.com, Chamber of Commerce listings, Sunbiz, BBB, local "best of" lists.
- ~60+ additional candidates were checked and **excluded** because they already have working websites.
- Categories that came up nearly dry (almost everyone has a site): boutiques, thrift/consignment, appliance repair, delis, ice cream shops, cafes, bars, bakeries, massage therapists, pest control, movers.
- Not fully swept before the research budget ran out: roofing, tile contractors, and a couple of MassageBook-only massage therapists (Quantum Massage Therapies, Sacred Space Massage) — worth a manual look.
- Entity-confusion warnings are flagged in notes where a similarly-named business elsewhere owns the "obvious" domain — that's also a selling point when pitching.
- Contact info marked "via Facebook/Yelp/Thumbtack" means the phone number isn't published outside that platform.
